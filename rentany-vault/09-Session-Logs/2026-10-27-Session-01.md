## 3. Why These Changes Were Necessary

1. Establishing the documentation second brain **first** ensures zero architectural ambiguities or costly rework during coding.
2. The user is a beginner; documenting the entire system in plain English alongside production-grade DDL and security models provides clarity and confidence.
3. High-value phone rentals face severe theft and fraud risks in the Indian context; establishing DigiLocker masking and concurrency locks before writing code prevents catastrophic financial losses.

---

## 4. Key Implementation Decisions & Alternatives Considered

- **Decision 1**: Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS for frontend over Next.js/React ([[Architecture-Decisions#ADR-002]]) for maximum mobile performance and zero framework churn.
- **Decision 2**: Upfront charge + automated instant deposit refund over Credit Card Pre-Authorization holds ([[Architecture-Decisions#ADR-007]]) due to 5-7 day RBI hold limits and 80%+ UPI adoption in Jabalpur.
- **Decision 3**: DigiLocker OKYC with strict `XXXX-XXXX-1234` masking ([[Architecture-Decisions#ADR-006]]) to maintain 100% legal compliance with UIDAI regulations.
- **Decision 4**: PostgreSQL `tstzrange` exclusion constraints for concurrency-safe hardware reservations ([[Database-Schema]]).
